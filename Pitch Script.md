# 🗣️ Pitch Script — Exactly What to Say

> You will freeze up if you improvise. So don't. Memorize this. It's short, honest, and gets to the point.
> See also: [[RJ Master Plan — One Rep a Day]] · [[Fire Drill Tracker]]

**The golden rule:** You are NOT begging. You are offering to fix a painful problem for money. That's a fair trade. Business owners *want* someone to make sense of their messy books.

---

## 👤 WALK-IN PITCH (your #1 method — free, just your feet)

**Bring:** 1 printed sample dashboard (print at a cafe, ~GHS 2–5). Smile. Speak slowly.

> **You (smile):** Good morning! I help small businesses turn their messy sales or stock records into a simple one-page dashboard — so you can see what's selling best, what's wasting money, and what to restock. I can show you in 30 seconds — may I?

*(If yes — show the printed sample, point to the charts)*

> **You:** This took me about a day to build from a shop's raw data. If you give me your Excel sheet, your notebook numbers, or even WhatsApp orders, I'll clean it and hand you back something like this in 2 days. It's **GHS 200** — and if it's not useful, you don't pay.

*(Pause. Let them think. Do NOT keep talking.)*

**Handling the 3 common responses:**

| They say… | You say… |
|---|---|
| *"How much again?"* | *"GHS 200 for the full dashboard, done in 2 days. Most shops tell me it pays for itself the first time they restock smart."* |
| *"Let me think about it."* | *"Of course — here's my number. I only take 2 shops per week so I can finish fast, so let me know by [day]."* (scarcity + deadline) |
| *"I don't have data on a computer."* | *"That's exactly why I help. Even a notebook or your WhatsApp — I'll type it up and build the dashboard. Bring me one week of sales, I'll show you a free sample of the first chart."* |
| *"No, not interested."* | *"No problem, thank you for your time! Have a great day."* (smile, leave, log it, next one) |

**Pricing flexibility (only if they're hesitating on price):**
- Tight shop? → *"I can do a smaller version for GHS 150."*
- Want to close NOW? → *"GHS 200, but if you pay today I'll throw in a second chart free."*

Do you know anyone who'd want this — or do you need it yourself? Even a notebook of sales works, I type it up. 🙏

*(Send the sample dashboard photo with it.)*

## ☎️ PHONE/CALL (use sparingly — costs airtime)

> "Good morning, my name is RJ. I build simple sales dashboards for shops like yours from your existing records — help you see what's really selling. It's GHS 200 and takes 2 days. Can I come by today or tomorrow to show you a sample?"

---

## 🧾 WHAT TO SAY ABOUT YOURSELF (when they ask "who are you?")

> "I'm RJ, a data analyst in Accra. I'm certified in data analytics and I help small businesses make sense of their numbers — turn raw records into clear decisions. No fancy software needed on your side."

**Keep it short.** You don't need to mention being broke, the Saudi dream, or anything else. You're a data analyst. That's the truth. Sell that.

---

## 🎯 THE 4 RULES OF SELLING (when you're scared)

1. **They can't hurt you.** The worst they say is "no." A "no" costs you nothing. Log it and walk to the next door.
2. **Smile + slow voice.** Nervous people talk fast and look down. Force yourself to speak slowly and look them in the eye. Confidence = slower.
3. **Stop talking after the price.** Say the price, then SHUT UP. The silence is where they decide. Filling it with nervous chatter kills the sale.
4. **10 before lunch, every day.** This is a numbers game. Pitch 10, expect 1–2 yes. The math is on your side by Day 3–4.
