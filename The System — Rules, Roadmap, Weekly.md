## ⚖️ Rules (the same ones, but permanent)

1. **One Project Rule.** Only one active project at a time. New ideas → Idea Vault.
2. **No Live Trading While Broke.** Until GHS 1,500 saved, no real money in XAUUSD. Study only.
3. **Reps Over Research.** 1 hour of doing > 10 hours of reading about doing.
4. **Pitch Before Polish.** Build rough → pitch → get feedback → improve.
5. **Daily Public Artifact.** One thing on GitHub or LinkedIn every day. Future employers are watching.
6. **Sunday Review.** Every Sunday 7pm, fill in Fire Drill Tracker scorecard.
7. **The Pivot Table Test.** Before any new tool/course/system, ask: *"Will this get me a paying client in 14 days?"* If no, it waits.
8. **The Body Rule.** Sleep 7 hours. Water 2L. Sunlight 15 min. One real meal.

## 🧠 Time management (the actual answer)

You don't have a calendar problem. You have a "do the next thing first" problem. So:
- One task before everything = the gate. Today's gate = pivot table.
- No YouTube, no research, no chat tools until the gate is done.
- After the gate, the rest of the day is for pitches and outreach (10/day per Door 1).
- After 7pm, stop. Sleep is a weapon.

That's the entire time management system. Don't build another one.
