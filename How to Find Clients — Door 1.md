# 🔎 How to Find Clients — Door 1 Edition

> **Goal:** Land 2 paying clients in 14 days to pay rent (GHS 500).
> **You don't have a "finding clients" problem — you have a "going outside and talking to humans" problem.**
> Pitch 10 businesses per day → 2 will say yes somewhere in 14 days. Math is on your side.
> See also: [[Pitch Script]] · [[Door 1 — First Pitch (held by hand)]] · [[Fire Drill Tracker]]

---

## 🥇 Way 1 — WALK-IN (your #1, start TOMORROW)

The most reliable cash path for a broke beginner in Accra. Your face + a printed paper + 10 shops per morning.

### Where to walk (pick one area per day, don't scatter)
- **Madina market** — dense, many small shops
- **Makola market area** — busy, big traders
- **Osu Oxford Street** — pharmacies, salons, restaurants
- **Tema Community 1 & 2** — minimarts, forex bureaus
- **Spintex Road** — pharmacies, hardware stores, importers
- **Achimota market** — traders, salons
- **East Legon / Dansoman** — higher-end shops (better prices)

### Who to walk into (target list — pick 10 per morning)
- Pharmacies / chemical shops ✅ BEST — they track sales but never analyze
- Minimarts / supermarkets ✅
- Forex bureaus ✅ HIGH value — they have lots of transaction data
- Salons / barbershops with many clients ✅
- Hardware stores ✅
- Phone repair / accessory shops ✅
- Event planners ✅
- Small importers ✅
- Churches (donations tracking) — ask the administrator, not the pastor
- Driving schools
- Local Instagram/TikTok sellers (DM them, don't walk in)

### How to walk in (the exact physical sequence)
1. Walk in. Smile. Don't look nervous.
2. Ask: *"Good morning — please, is the owner or manager around?"*
3. When they come, say the opener (see [[Pitch Script]]).
4. Show the printed dashboard. Point at the top-products chart.
5. Say the price: *"GHS 200, and if it's not useful, you don't pay."*
6. **SHUT UP.** 5 seconds of silence. Let them decide.
7. Log the response in your phone notes the second you walk out.

### Tactical tips
- **Best times:** Tue–Fri, 9–11am. Monday mornings shops are busy opening. Friday afternoon is relaxed.
- **Avoid:** Rush hours (no time to talk), lunch (they're eating), markets on market days (too chaotic).
- **Dress:** clean shirt, neat trousers, deodorant. You don't need a suit. You need to look like someone they'd let touch their numbers.
- **Carry:** printed dashboard + pen + small notebook + phone with your sample dashboard PDF.
- **Pitch in pairs if possible:** if your fear is huge, grab a friend. They don't talk — you do. They're just moral support.

## 🥈 Way 2 — YOUR PHONE CONTACTS (free, fastest trust)

Your contacts already trust you. Use that.

### The exercise (15 min tonight)
1. Open your WhatsApp/phone contacts.
2. Scroll. Mark EVERY person who:
   - Owns a business or side hustle
   - Manages a shop, church, school, NGO
   - Sells things on Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp status
   - Is an accountant, secretary, or manager somewhere
3. You probably have 15–30 such contacts. List them in your notebook.

### The message (copy-paste from [[Pitch Script]])
> *"Hi [Name]! I'm helping small businesses this month turn their messy sales/stock records into a clean dashboard — best sellers, what's wasting money, what to restock. GHS 200, done in 2 days. Know anyone who needs it — or do you?"*

### Send to all 15–30 tonight, in batches of 5.
- Half won't reply. That's normal.
- 3–5 will refer someone. THAT's your pipeline.
- 1–2 might buy directly.

### Follow-up rule
If no reply in 2 days, send one polite nudge:
> *"Hi again — just following up in case it got buried. If you or anyone you know wants a sales dashboard, I'm taking 2 clients this week. Let me know!"*

Then drop it. Don't spam.

## 🏅 Way 4 — REFERRALS (starts after client #1)

Once you deliver your first dashboard, ask that client:
> *"Happy with it? Great — if you refer me to one other business owner, your next dashboard is GHS 100 instead of 200."*

**Why this works:** Every pharmacy owner knows 3 other pharmacy owners. One client → 3 leads → your pipeline fills itself.

### After 3 clients, ask each for:
- A short written testimonial (2–3 sentences) — for your portfolio + upsideonly.com
- A screenshot of their dashboard with their numbers hidden — for your sample portfolio
- A Google review (Way 4 above)

## 🧠 MINDSET — read when fear hits

> "No" costs you nothing. "Yes" pays your rent.
