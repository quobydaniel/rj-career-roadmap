## 🚪 DOOR 1 — FIRE DRILL (Days 1–14)

**Goal:** Pay rent (GHS 500). Nothing else.
**Hard freeze:** No Jarvis, no YouTube, no kids class, no new courses, no new repos, no live trading.
**Only exception:** keep `upsideonly.com` as a 1-page placeholder.

> ⚡ **DUAL-PURPOSE RULE** — every Door 1 client also feeds your [[Career Stack — One Action, Three Returns]]:
> 1. Cash (rent)
> 2. Anonymized dashboard → GitHub commit (portfolio)
> 3. LinkedIn post + connection requests (recruiter bait)
> This is how Door 1 builds the Saudi job. One action, triple return.

### The math (this is why you should not panic)

| Path | Math | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2 dashboard jobs | GHS 250 × 2 | **GHS 500 ✅** |
| 1 dashboard + gigs | GHS 250 + GHS 250 gig work | **GHS 500 ✅** |
| 5 tiny cleanups | GHS 100 × 5 | **GHS 500 ✅** |

**Your rent = 2 to 4 people saying yes. Pitch 10/day → by day 3–4, someone says yes.**

### What you're selling (ONE thing only)

Don't sell "data analytics." Sell this:

> *"I'll take your messy sales/stock book (Excel, notebook, or WhatsApp), clean it, and give you back a 1-page dashboard showing your best-selling items, your worst, and where money leaks — so you know what to buy more of. Done in 2 days."*

**Price:** GHS 150–400 per job (start GHS 200–250, you can do this).

### Who to approach (Accra targets — pick 20)

Pharmacies, small supermarkets/minimarts, forex bureaus, real estate agents, barbershops/salons with many clients, churches (donations tracking), small importers, event planners, local Instagram sellers, driving schools.

### Outreach method (LOW DATA — your reality)

- **Primary = in-person walk-ins.** Free. Just your feet + one printed sample dashboard.
- **Secondary = WhatsApp** to people you already know (free on WiFi). Not strangers.
- **One-time prep:** Print a sample 1-page dashboard at a cafe (~GHS 2–5). Carry it everywhere. A picture closes sales; 1,000 WhatsApp messages don't.

### Daily rhythm (14 days straight)

| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| 8:00–11:00 | **Outreach.** Walk in / WhatsApp / call. Target **10 contacts before lunch.** Log every one in `Fire Drill Tracker.md`. |
| 11:00–15:00 | **Do the work** for whoever said yes. Deliver same/next day. Get paid. |
| 15:00–18:00 | **Any gig that pays this week** (helping, data entry, whatever). Cash = rent. |
| 18:00–19:00 | Update `Fire Drill Tracker.md` — leads, results, money in. |
| After 19:00 | **REST.** Sleep is a weapon. You cannot grind 16 hrs for 14 days without breaking. |

| Item | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Print 1 sample dashboard | ~GHS 2–5 | Show, don't tell. Closes sales. |
| Airtime (minimal) | ~GHS 10–20 | Only for calls that matter. Use free WhatsApp otherwise. |

1. Drop price to GHS 150/job — volume over margin.
2. Message 5 people you KNOW (family, former classmates, your landlord's contacts): *"I'm doing cheap data cleanup this week, GHS 150, who needs it?"*

4. **Last resort:** transparent conversation with landlord — *"I have GHS X now, balance by [date], here's my plan."* Most landlords prefer a plan + partial payment over silence.

**Goal:** Go from "theoretically good" → "here's proof I can do it."
**Rule:** ONE project at a time. Build, finish, document, THEN start the next.

- Run the strategy over real historical data. Produce a **clean report**: win rate, profit factor, max drawdown, equity curve, 3 charts.
- **Output:** a polished PDF + a GitHub repo with a great README. STUDY ONLY — no live money.
- **Learn as you go:** pandas, matplotlib, basic backtesting. Only what THIS report needs. No courses.

- **Output:** a case study page on your portfolio + the dataset on GitHub.
- **Why this matters:** it's *directly* relevant to Saudi employers (oil & gas safety analytics).

### Project 3 (Weeks 7–8): Portfolio + upsideonly.com
- Polish `quoby-rj-portfolio` (React/Vite/TS — already built!) to host Projects 1 & 2.
- Put `upsideonly.com` live as your professional storefront.
- **Output:** a real, public portfolio URL you can put on a CV and LinkedIn.
- **Now you're practically good — with proof.**
