## 🧠 The mindset shift that changes everything

**You think:** "I don't know how to build it, so I can't sell it."
**The truth:** None of your clients know how to build a dashboard either.
That's literally why they pay you.

A plumber doesn't refuse to take a job because he hasn't seen THAT exact
toilet before. He knows the general shape of toilets. You know the general
shape of dashboards. The client's data is just a different toilet. You'll
figure it out on the job — with me as your backup.

**The fear is not about skill. It's about not wanting to feel stupid if they
ask a question you can't answer. The fix:** print the sample, point at the
charts, and if they ask something you don't know, say:

> *"Good question — let me check the data on that and get back to you tomorrow. That's exactly why I take 2 days."*

That answer is professional, not weak. It buys you time AND looks like
thoroughness. Win-win.

## 🛡️ The 3 questions you're scared of (and the answers)

### Q1: "Can you do it for my actual business?"
**A:** *"Yes. This is a sample from another pharmacy. For you, I take YOUR sales records — Excel, notebook, even WhatsApp screenshots — and I build YOUR version of this. Same idea, your numbers."*

### Q2: "How do I know you won't just take my GHS 200 and disappear?"
**A:** *"You don't pay until you see it. I build it first, show it to you, and if it's not useful you don't pay. The risk is on me, not you."*
(This is HUGE. It removes 90% of their fear. And you'll deliver because you and I will build it together.)

### Q3: "I don't have my data on a computer — it's in a notebook."
**A:** *"That's exactly why I help. Bring me one week, or a photo of one page of sales. I'll type it up and show you a free sample of the first chart. If you like it, we do the full month for GHS 200."*
(The free first chart is your secret weapon. They can't say no to free.)

## 🗺️ Your Day 1 — minute by minute

### Tonight (before bed — 15 min)
- [ ] Open the xlsx in LibreOffice Calc / Excel. Look at the Dashboard sheet. Print it. ONE page. Color if possible (~GHS 2–5 at a cafe).
- [ ] Read this note + the Pitch Script out loud once.

### Tomorrow 7:00–7:30am
- [ ] Eat something small. You need energy.
- [ ] Pick a street/business area with 10+ small shops. Pharmacies, minimarts, forex bureaus, salons, hardware stores.
- [ ] Put the printed dashboard in a folder or clean envelope. Carry a pen + a small notebook.

### 8:00–11:00am — THE PITCH (10 businesses)
For each:
1. Walk in. Smile. Ask: *"Good morning, please is the owner or manager around?"*
2. When they arrive, say the opener (from Pitch Script).
3. Show the printed dashboard. Point at the charts. Explain 1–2 rows.
4. Say the price: *"GHS 200, and if it's not useful you don't pay."*
5. **SHUT UP.** 5-second silence. Let them decide.
6. Whatever they say — log it in your phone notes immediately after walking out.

### 11:00am–3:00pm — DELIVER (if you got a yes)
- Take their data (USB, photo, notebook page).
- Come home. Open Obsidian/WhatsApp me.
- YOU AND I build their dashboard together that afternoon.
- Deliver it tomorrow. Collect GHS 200.

### 7:00pm — LOG
- Open Obsidian → `Fire Drill Tracker.md`
- Fill in: every business, every response, every GHS in.
- Then come back and tell me how it went.
